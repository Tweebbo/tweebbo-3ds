# Tweebbo for Nintendo 3DS 1.3.10

Optional music and room update. Keep playing and update whenever convenient.

- The Jukebox can queue the 72 original Kepler Classic cartridge loops. A track added on PC plays on this console, and the console playlist can add the same tracks.
- Room Trax songs saved by anyone in the room can be added from the console. YouTube and Spotify links remain PC and phone only.
- Room music now uses the original interface sounds for a new console message, a sent message and credits received.
- Wall posters, paintings and other wall items stay on the visible wall face while placing or moving them.
- Avatar rendering checks a validated cache slot before scanning the 256-entry avatar cache. Idle head tracking computes nearby avatar positions once per draw instead of once per neighbor.
- In the 50-player emulator fixture the scene median is about 5% lower on Old and New 3DS. Tail and p95 are not consistently better, so this is not a claim that the reported join stutter is fixed. Physical-console acceptance remains pending.
- Nothing else changes in rooms, games, saves or your account.

Install with the in-game updater or FBI, or replace the 3DSX in the Homebrew Launcher. Keep your saved data and reopen Tweebbo after installation. The complete package is available.
