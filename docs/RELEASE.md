Tweebbo for Nintendo 3DS: the same hotel as [tweebbo.lol](https://tweebbo.lol), on your console. Play with people on the web, on X and on PC.

> Tweebbo is in development and may be unstable.

**Fixed in 1.1.0:** the Lido rooms (The Pool, the Diving Deck and Rooftop Rumble) no longer open as a black, empty room. Their layered scenery packs into two large pages that no longer fit in the console's texture memory; the pages are now stored in a smaller format and the room releases the previous room's background before loading them.

**Install (CFW with Luma3DS required)**
1. On the console, open **FBI** → *Remote Install* → *Scan QR Code* and scan the QR code below (`qr.png` in this release), or download `tweebbo.cia` and install it with FBI.
2. Open Tweebbo from the HOME Menu. New versions are offered inside the game (*Update now*), so you only install it by hand once.

`tweebbo.3dsx` is for the Homebrew Launcher (`sdmc:/3ds/tweebbo.3dsx`).
