Tweebbo for Nintendo 3DS: the same hotel as [tweebbo.lol](https://tweebbo.lol), on your console. Play with people on the web, on PC and on Nintendo Switch and PlayStation Vita.

> Tweebbo is in development and may be unstable.

**New in 1.3.0**
- Play Fast Food, Battle Ball and Snow Storm from Game Center, including practice and online matches with browser, Switch and Vita players.
- Visit the classic Battle Ball and Snow Storm lobbies, also listed in offline navigation. Saved rooms that no longer exist return you to reception.
- Choose English, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, French or Brazilian Portuguese from Help / Language.
- Fast Food has more detailed textures and smoother scaling within the same 4 MiB texture budget. Games release their textures and audio when closed.
- Update downloads can use two connections, reuse connections and resume interrupted transfers with SHA-256 verification.

Host and emulator checks passed for the game ports and download engine. Physical-console performance, sound and suspend/resume have not been certified.

**New in 1.2.5**
- Stack mode for room builders: type `:stackmode 1` in chat to stack furniture on top of anything, `:stackmode 2` to place items in the same floor space, and `:stackmode 0` to turn it off. Items can be picked up or moved from the middle of a stack.
- Room layouts P, Q, R, S, X and Z show a proper doorway in the wall.

**New in 1.2.4**
- Catalogue: long pages scroll with the stylus again, so every item can be reached and bought. The Hand and trade lists scroll the same way.
- The hotel now has favourite rooms and a public changelog at [tweebbo.lol/changelog](https://tweebbo.lol/changelog).

**New in 1.2.3**
- Doorbell rooms: ring to enter, and let visitors in from your own room.
- Choose the badge shown first on your profile, and open Tweebbo Club from the catalogue.
- Improved furniture animations and restored bear, terrier and pig textures, coat colours and shadows.

**New in 1.2.2**
- F3 reception in all four regions, PC-style featured-room ordering and the expanded furniture catalogue.
- Lia opens Suggestions, with validation, retained drafts and retry support.
- Wave, kiss, laugh, thumbs-up and sign keep the avatar seated.
- Official pixel-art T and yellow Donate button in the bottom-screen header.

Verified with native host tests and a LAN-only Azahar build. Physical 3DS validation remains pending.

**New in 1.2.1**
- Payment screens no longer show a generic website QR before a code exists. Donate / Pay by phone now offers crypto and custom donation amounts on the phone, and the Pay / QR button and text fit completely.
- The catalogue is re-exported from the current PC hotel: 2,097 definitions, including the Exchange chip tokens and the newest furniture ranges.
- The hotel now supports the Igloo with its penguin visitors, room names and personal missions, the 30-minute AFK pause, room deletion and the other recent PC features.
- Input, chat history, beds, casino panels and network polling follow the current shared console code.

**New in 1.2.0**
- Offline play now includes the Classic Lobby, Classic Park, both pool areas, Rooftop Rumble, Casino and After Hours Bar, with bots.
- Support and chips payment by QR code from the console.
- Player cards now show the correct console (Nintendo 3DS, Nintendo Switch or PlayStation Vita).
- In-game updates resume interrupted downloads instead of sending you to FBI.

**Install (CFW with Luma3DS required)**
1. On the console, open **FBI** → *Remote Install* → *Scan QR Code* and scan the QR code below (`qr.png` in this release), or download `tweebbo.cia` and install it with FBI.
2. Open Tweebbo from the HOME Menu. New versions are offered inside the game (*Update now*), so you only install it by hand once.

`tweebbo.3dsx` is for the Homebrew Launcher (`sdmc:/3ds/tweebbo.3dsx`).
